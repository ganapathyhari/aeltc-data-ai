Decision Support These are considerations derived from the analytical findings. They are not automatic recommendations. All operational decisions remain with the AELTC operations team.
Premium ticket holders arrive significantly earlier
Gate Management

Insight

Centre Court Debenture holders arrive a median of ~130 minutes before first serve — 55 minutes earlier than Grounds Pass holders and 55 minutes earlier than Queue Ticket holders. This gradient is statistically robust (ANOVA F=42,674, p<0.001).

Evidence

OLS coef: −11.6 min per tier step
Premium median: ~130 min
General median: ~95 min
Queue median: ~75 min

Operational consideration

Premium gate lanes and hospitality areas may benefit from earlier staffing. A staggered staff deployment could align with the observed tier-based arrival gradient rather than a single all-gates-open time.

International visitors arrive ~18 minutes earlier
Transport & Communications

Insight

International visitors arrive measurably earlier than UK domestic visitors across all tier groups. The effect is consistent and statistically significant (Welch t-test t=217.8, p<0.001).

Evidence

OLS coef: −17.9 min (international)
48% of visitors are international
55% of Debenture holders are international
Effect consistent across all tiers

Operational consideration

On days with higher international attendance (opening rounds, high-profile draws), earlier gate pressure may be expected. Transport partner communications and queue management activation could be timed to reflect this.

Rain days shift arrivals ~31–35 minutes later
Weather Contingency

Insight

On rain days, the median arrival time shifts approximately 31–35 minutes later. The effect is consistent across all tier groups. On roofed courts (Centre Court, No.1 Court) this may be less pronounced as matches can proceed.

Evidence

OLS coef: +34.5 min (rain day)
Welch t-test: t=−365.8, p<0.001
30% of Championship days have rain
Effect holds across all tiers

Operational consideration

When forecast rain is expected, gate-staff peak deployment times could be shifted later. A weather-contingency staffing plan with two schedule variants (dry day / rain day) could be prepared in advance for each match day.

Temperature has a small but significant effect on timing
Match-Day Planning

Insight

Each degree Celsius increase in temperature is associated with visitors arriving approximately 0.95 minutes earlier. While individually small, a 10°C hotter day is associated with ~10 minutes earlier median arrival.

Evidence

OLS coef: −0.95 min per °C
p<0.001 (statistically significant)
Warm/hot days: Cold/Cool days
Temp range observed: 10–35°C

Operational consideration

Hot days could be incorporated into arrival timing models for gate planning. Combined with the rain effect, the model allows a two-variable weather lookup for operational planning purposes.

Real-time fan-out data creates operational awareness
Staffing & Communications

Insight

The real-time notification infrastructure (fan-out pipeline) tracks ~98,000 follower engagements and delivers match events in 2–6.6 seconds. This creates a real-time picture of which matches are drawing the most audience attention.

Evidence

Centre Court: 60,020 followers
Delivery time: 2.0–6.6s full fan-out
Scenario-3 test harness measurements
Live Confluent Cloud infrastructure

Operational consideration

High-engagement match events could trigger operational alerts — for instance, a completed set on Centre Court may drive a secondary crowd movement to big screens or food areas. Real-time engagement data could supplement historical arrival predictions.

Production query (watsonx.data Presto)

Reference
-- Arrival analysis join: Teamcard × Occupancy × Weather (2026 Championships)
SELECT
    t.DateTime            AS scan_ts,
    t."Entrance.Ref"      AS gate_id,
    t."Block.Ref"         AS ticket_block,
    t.PriceClass          AS price_class,
    o.report_on_name      AS zone,
    o.occupancy,
    o.utilisation_pct,
    o.queue_depth,
    o.wait_time_seconds,
    o.gates_open_t0_min,
    w."Air Temperature"   AS temp_c,
    w.Humidity,
    w.Rain                AS precip_mm
FROM   iceberg.aeltc_gold.teamcard_scans         t
JOIN   iceberg.aeltc_gold.occupancy_timeseries   o
       ON  DATE(t.DateTime) = o.event_date
       AND HOUR(t.DateTime) = HOUR(o.timestamp_local)
JOIN   iceberg.aeltc_gold.weather_station        w
       ON  DATE(t.DateTime) = DATE(w.timestamp)
       AND HOUR(t.DateTime) = HOUR(w.timestamp)
WHERE  o.day_is_champs = true
  AND  t.DateTime BETWEEN TIMESTAMP '2026-06-29 00:00:00'
                      AND TIMESTAMP '2026-07-12 23:59:59'
